Carney say Trump started trade war with fresh tarrifs 'attack'

Canada's Prime Minister, in a recent address, revealed that his country was compelled to impose retaliatory tariffs following the United States' decision to levy fresh tariffs on Canadian imports. This move, he asserts, was not a choice Canada arrived at lightly but a necessary response to what he describes as an 'attack' in the guise of trade tariffs initiated by President Trump. The Prime Minister expressed his disappointment, noting that diplomatic channels had been pursued in an attempt to resolve trade disputes amicably. However, faced with escalating measures from the US administration, he declared that Canada had no choice but to defend its economic interests. He pointed out that the US tariffs were unjustified and harmful to both countries' economies, potentially affecting thousands of jobs on both sides of the border. The Prime Minister's remarks came at a time when tensions between the two nations seemed to be at an all-time high, with the global markets watching closely. Observers note that the repercussions of this trade conflict could have far-reaching impacts, potentially altering the landscape of North American trade for the foreseeable future as both nations dig in their heels in this developing trade war.
