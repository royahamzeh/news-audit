US hits three Iranian oil tankers after saying its warships were targeted

US-Iran Tensions Escalate

Recent conflicts between the United States and Iran have intensified after the US hit three Iranian oil tankers. These strikes were in response to reports of US warships being targeted by Iranian forces.

Background

  • Period of relative calm disrupted.
  • US cites self-defense for its actions.
  • Iran condemns US strikes as unjustified aggression.

Global Implications

The military exchange between these two nations has alarmed international observers and raised concerns about further escalation in an already unstable region. Iran's promise of retaliation adds to the mounting tensions.
