IVF staff accused of misleading UK parents about sperm and egg donors in northern Cyprus

IVF Scandal in Northern Cyprus

  • Several UK parents have accused northern Cyprus IVF clinics of using the wrong sperm and egg donors.
  • Parents were misled about the donors matching their specifications.
  • This has led to significant emotional distress and ethical concerns for the families involved.
  • The situation highlights the need for stricter regulations and transparency in international IVF services.
  • Parents are demanding thorough investigations and accountability from the clinics.
