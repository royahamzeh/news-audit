About 60,000 migrants arrive in Ceuta in 24 hours, Spanish territory's leader says

Migrant Surge Overwhelms Ceuta

  • Ceuta saw an unprecedented arrival of about 60,000 migrants in 24 hours.
  • This number is an increase of over 70% relative to Ceuta's population of 83,600.
  • The migrants originated from various African nations.
  • Local authorities are struggling with the logistical challenges posed by the influx.

Immediate Reactions and Challenges

  • The Spanish government is in talks with the EU for assistance.
  • Ceuta's leader calls for international cooperation to manage the situation.
