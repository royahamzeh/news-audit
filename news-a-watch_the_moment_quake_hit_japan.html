Watch: The moment quake hit Japan

On July 28, a powerful earthquake struck Japan's Kyushu island, causing widespread panic and disruption. Videos from across the region captured the terrifying moment when the quake hit, showcasing the immediate reactions of residents and the chaos that ensued. The earthquake, which registered a significant magnitude, caused buildings to sway and items to fall off shelves as people scrambled for safety. Emergency services were immediately deployed to assess damage and provide assistance to those affected. Fortunately, despite the intensity of the tremor, there were no immediate reports of serious injuries or fatalities. The Japanese government and local authorities quickly issued warnings and updates, urging people to stay alert as aftershocks were anticipated. The event once again highlighted Japan's vulnerability to natural disasters, a country that has made significant advancements in earthquake preparedness and early warning systems. However, the emotional toll on the residents is undeniable, as many were reminded of past tragedies. As the situation unfolds, videos of the earthquake serve as a sobering reminder of the power and unpredictability of nature. While the region's infrastructure generally held up well, the quake's psychological impact lingers as recovery and evaluation efforts are in full swing.
