More than 100 aftershocks hit Japan after powerful quake that killed 13

Japan on High Alert Following Devastating Earthquake

  • More than 100 aftershocks have followed an initial magnitude 7.4 earthquake.
  • The quake has resulted in 13 fatalities and widespread damage.
  • Authorities warn of potential aftershocks reaching up to magnitude 7.

Emergency Response and Public Advisory

  • Emergency kits and safety measures are being advised for all citizens.
  • Information disseminated widely to keep the public informed.
  • Evacuation centers and hospitals are on high alert.

Government and Public Cooperation

  • Efforts continue to provide support and resources.
  • Structural assessments are being prioritized for safety.
  • Community resilience plays a crucial role in managing the crisis.
