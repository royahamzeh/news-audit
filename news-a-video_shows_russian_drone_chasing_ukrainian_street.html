Video shows Russian drone chasing Ukrainian street vendor in 'human safari' attack

In a disturbing incident that highlights the ongoing perils in Ukraine, a video has emerged showing a Russian drone relentlessly pursuing a Ukrainian street vendor in what has been described as a 'human safari' attack. This shocking footage, which Ukraine has condemned as a blatant war crime, underscores the brutal conditions faced by civilians in the conflict zone. The video captures the chilling moment when the vendor, upon spotting the drone, begins to flee in terror. Despite their desperate attempt to evade capture, the drone continues its harrowing chase. Commentators and officials have expressed outrage over this inhumane tactic, emphasizing its psychological warfare aspect. Ukraine's government has urged international bodies to take immediate action against what it views as clear violations of humanitarian law. The use of technology in such a manner raises ethical questions about the conduct of nations during warfare and the protection of civilian lives. This incident adds to a series of reported abuses and further strains the already tenuous relationship between the warring countries. Describing the attack as a 'human safari' sheds light on the calculated cruelty of deploying a drone for targeting unarmed civilians, turning them into prey in their own homeland.
