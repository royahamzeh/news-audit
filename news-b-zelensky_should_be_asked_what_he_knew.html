Zelensky should be asked what he knew about government corruption, sacked minister tells BBC

Former Minister Calls for Accountability and Elections

Mykhailo Fedorov, a former Ukrainian minister, has urged President Volodymyr Zelensky to clarify his awareness of government corruption issues. In conversation with the BBC, Fedorov stressed this as a necessary step for ensuring transparency in leadership, particularly amidst the ongoing conflict with Russia.

Key Points

  • Fedorov calls on Zelensky to address corruption issues.
  • Advocacy for wartime elections to maintain democratic processes.
  • Challenges highlighted in conducting elections during ongoing conflict.
