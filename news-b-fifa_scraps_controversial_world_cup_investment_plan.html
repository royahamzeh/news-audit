Fifa Scraps Controversial Investment Plan

Fifa, the global body for football, has decided to drop its plans to sell stakes in major competitions after encountering substantial opposition. The proposal, pushed by Fifa president Gianni Infantino, aimed to finance selling shares in events, sparking a backlash from clubs, leagues, and national associations. Critics argued that this approach could undermine the sport's integrity.

Key Points

  • The investment plan received criticism from various stakeholders.
  • Concerns focused on maintaining control and integrity.
  • Fifa emphasizes the balance between innovation and tradition.
