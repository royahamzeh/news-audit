At least 132 killed in Colombia's largest earthquake in years

In a devastating turn of events, Colombia has been rocked by its most severe earthquake in recent years, resulting in at least 132 fatalities. The quake's impact was widespread, with over 480 individuals reported injured. Rescue teams are working tirelessly, fearing the numbers could rise as many are still believed to be trapped under the debris of collapsed buildings. The earthquake struck with a force that was felt across several regions, causing panic and chaos among residents. Immediate emergency response efforts have been deployed, with local and international aid agencies racing against time to provide relief and medical assistance to those affected. The government has declared a state of emergency, mobilizing resources to assist in rescue missions and offer support to the grieving families. The incident has overwhelmed hospital facilities in the areas hit hardest, prompting urgent calls for medical supplies and personnel. Analysts have begun assessing the economic impact, which is expected to be substantial, given the scale of destruction. This catastrophic event has drawn global attention, with messages of condolence and offers of support pouring in from countries worldwide. As Colombia begins to grapple with the aftermath, the focus remains on rescue efforts and providing relief to the affected communities.
