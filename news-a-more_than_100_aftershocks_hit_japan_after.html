More than 100 aftershocks hit Japan after powerful quake that killed 13

Japan is on high alert after experiencing more than 100 aftershocks following a devastating earthquake that left 13 people dead. The initial quake, which struck at a magnitude of 7.4, caused significant damage across the region, prompting emergency services to work around the clock providing aid to affected residents. While the earthquake itself has passed, the threat remains high as officials warn that aftershocks could potentially reach magnitudes of up to 7. Citizens are being urged to remain vigilant and prepared for further seismic activity. Emergency protocols are being recommended, including keeping emergency kits ready and knowing safe spots within homes. Authorities have been disseminating information on safety measures through various channels to ensure the public is well informed. Hospitals are on standby, and evacuation centers have been set up to accommodate those displaced by the quake. Structural assessments are ongoing as engineers determine the safety of buildings and infrastructure. The government has assured the public of their commitment to providing support and resources as the nation grapples with this natural disaster. As rescue and relief operations continue, the resilience and cooperation of the Japanese people are proving crucial in managing the aftermath of this catastrophic event.
