Instagram accounts fuelling Ceuta crisis with paid advice for help to cross

Top Comment Podcast Investigation

The BBC's Top Comment podcast has revealed a disturbing trend on social media, specifically on Instagram, where certain accounts are promoting illegal border crossings.

Ceuta: A Gateway to Europe

These Instagram profiles are providing paid advice to migrants looking to cross into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta from Morocco. They use glamorous narratives to portray the dangerous swim around the border fence as an adventurous challenge.
