Watch: Why rescuers fear new barrier lake could cause another Nepal-Tibet flood

Concerns Over New Barrier Lake

Following a significant landslide near the Nepal-Tibet border, a barrier lake has formed, causing fears of another flood. This natural dam, created by the recent heavy rains, is unstable and poses a threat to nearby communities.

Key Points

  • The lake was formed by a landslide, blocking a river with rocks and debris.
  • The rising water increases the risk of the dam breaking and a flood occurring.
  • Rescuers and experts are monitoring the situation closely and considering solutions.
  • Authorities are warning downstream communities of potential evacuation needs.

Current Strategy

Experts are examining the barrier lake's stability and exploring engineering alternatives to prevent a catastrophic flood event. The focus is on swift intervention to protect human lives and minimize environmental impact.
