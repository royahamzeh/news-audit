Mistrial declared in Lindsay Clancy murder case, after jury deadlocks

A mistrial has been declared in the highly publicized Lindsay Clancy murder case after the jury was unable to reach a unanimous verdict. With the jury unable to find consensus, the decision now lies with the prosecution on whether to attempt a retrial. The aftermath of this case leaves Clancy's future in limbo and her case in a state of uncertainty as her legal team evaluates potential defenses for any impending court trials.
