Powerful 7.7-magnitude earthquake kills at least 38 in Indonesia

Deadly Earthquake in Eastern Indonesia

  • A powerful 7.7-magnitude earthquake has hit Flores, eastern Indonesia.
  • Currently, at least 38 people are confirmed dead.
  • Rescue teams are conducting operations to find survivors.
  • Severe infrastructure damage reported; many buildings flattened.
  • Continued aftershocks are affecting rescue efforts.
  • Government has deployed emergency response teams to the island.

Government and Community Response

  • Hospitals are overwhelmed, with makeshift facilities set up for the influx of injured people.
  • Survivors have been relocated to temporary shelters.
  • The National Disaster Mitigation Agency is actively coordinating relief efforts.
  • Residents are advised to remain alert for potential aftershocks.
