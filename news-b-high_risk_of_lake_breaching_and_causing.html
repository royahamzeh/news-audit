'High risk' of lake breaching and causing second flood, warns China

Potential Lake Breach Threatens Second Flood Near Nepal-Tibet Border

Authorities in China have issued a warning about a lake that was formed due to a recent landslide near the Nepal-Tibet border, highlighting the risk of it breaching and causing a second flood in the area.

  • The landslide has led to the obstruction of a river, resulting in the formation of a lake.
  • This newly formed body of water is putting pressure on makeshift natural barriers.
  • Failure of these barriers could lead to widespread flooding and impact local communities.

Emergency response teams have been deployed to monitor the situation, and evacuation plans are being prepared for residents living nearby. Local governments are on high alert, ready to respond swiftly, should the situation worsen. Environmental groups are raising concerns about the frequency of natural disasters in the region, possibly intensified by climate change.
