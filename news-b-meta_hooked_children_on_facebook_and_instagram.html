Meta hooked children on Facebook and Instagram, court hears

Meta Faces Allegations in Court

Meta Platforms, the parent company of Facebook and Instagram, stands accused in a trial involving multiple US states. The states allege that Meta deliberately targeted children and adolescents to make them excessively use their platforms.

Meta's Defense

Meta, however, argues that social media addiction does not have a scientific basis, dismissing the claims that their platforms addict users.

Key Arguments

  • Meta contends that their platforms provide overwhelmingly positive benefits, especially during tough global circumstances.
  • The states claim that the excessive use of social media has harmful effects on mental health, particularly for younger users.

Implications

The trial outcome could heavily influence future regulations related to social media use and its impact on minors.
