Nepal Rescuers Use Explosives to Save Trapped Workers

The situation in Nepal has become dire as rescuers employ explosives in a bid to reach workers trapped by widespread flooding. Families anxiously await any news as the region faces a mounting death toll of 939.

Key Points

  • Rescuers are blasting through a hillside to access trapped hydropower workers.
  • The catastrophic flood has caused severe landslides, complicating rescue efforts.
  • Families remain on edge, hoping for positive news amid growing fatalities.

Background

The workers were involved in a hydropower project when the floods hit, causing landslides that isolated them from safety. Rescuers initially used traditional methods but have now switched to explosive charges to clear debris faster.

Current Efforts

Authorities decided to use explosives due to difficult terrain and unstable ground, which have impeded progress. International teams are on alert, ready to jump in as needed.

Emotional Toll

The scene is emotionally charged, with families gathered nearby, desperate for news. Concerns about the safety measures in place for such projects have been raised.
