What we know so far about the Berlin Pride ramming attack

A tragic incident unfolded in Berlin on Saturday night when a suspect drove a vehicle into a crowd during a Pride event, causing chaos and prompting police to launch an immediate manhunt. The attack occurred at around 8 PM local time, as participants and onlookers were gathered in celebration of Berlin's annual Pride festivities. Witnesses reported scenes of panic and confusion as the car plowed through individuals, leaving several injured. Emergency services rushed to the scene, tending to the wounded and assessing the overall situation. Initial reports suggest that multiple people sustained injuries of varying severity, though fortunately, no fatalities have been confirmed at this time. The motive behind the attack remains unclear, but authorities are investigating all possible angles, including potential hate crime charges. The police have sealed off the area to conduct thorough investigations and are appealing to the public for any information that could lead to the suspect's capture. Berlin's mayor condemned the act, expressing solidarity with the LGBTQ+ community and pledging support to ensure their safety. This incident has cast a shadow over the Pride celebrations, but community leaders urge resilience and unity in the face of adversity.
