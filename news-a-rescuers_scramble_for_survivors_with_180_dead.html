Rescuers scramble for survivors with 180 dead in Colombia earthquake

Colombia faced a devastating earthquake that has left at least 180 people dead and scores trapped under rubble. As the earth shook violently, buildings crumbled, leaving cities in chaos and communities in urgent need of aid. Rescue teams are working around the clock, seeking to find survivors underneath the debris. Aftershocks continued to rattle the nation on Tuesday, adding to the already anxious atmosphere and hampering rescue missions. Emergency responders, equipped with specialized tools, have been joined by volunteers in the frantic effort to save lives. Despite facing challenges such as restricted access to certain areas and unstable structures, the spirit of resilience among the rescuers and local population endures. International communities have begun sending support, offering technical assistance and humanitarian aid to help Colombia navigate through this crisis. Hospitals are overflowing with injured victims, and medical staff are tirelessly working to provide care. Shelters have been set up for those who have lost their homes, providing essential supplies such as food, water, and blankets. As the nation comes to grips with the magnitude of this disaster, efforts to rescue, recover, and rebuild continue with an unyielding determination, bringing hope amid the tragedy and fostering unity in the face of immense adversity.
