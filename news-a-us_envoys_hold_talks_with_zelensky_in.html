US envoys hold talks with Zelensky in Kyiv after meeting Putin

US envoys conducted diplomatic talks with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky in Kyiv after previously engaging in discussions with Russian President Vladimir Putin. Steve Witkoff, who leads the US delegation, expressed a sense of encouragement following what he described as "substantive" discussions. These diplomatic engagements form part of ongoing efforts to bring about a de-escalation of tensions between the two countries. During their meeting, the US team reportedly discussed several key issues pertinent to the ongoing conflict, focusing on potential paths to a peaceful resolution. Witkoff emphasized the importance of these discussions, stating that the exchanges were both detailed and constructive, reflecting a shared interest in addressing the underlying causes of the current situation. The talks in Kyiv came after similar discussions in Moscow, both aimed at assessing the possibilities of reducing hostilities and fostering a stable environment. The US delegates were keen to understand the perspectives of both nations directly from their leaders, enhancing bilateral communication channels. The US team plans to continue these diplomatic efforts, hoping to contribute towards a cooperative solution that respects the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Ukraine while addressing mutual security concerns of all involved parties. Such efforts signal a renewed focus on dialogue amidst ongoing tensions.
