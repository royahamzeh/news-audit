Carney says US trade war 'will come at a cost' as Canada strikes back on tariffs

Canada Retaliates with Tariffs Amid US Trade War

Amid escalating trade tensions highlighted by the US imposing tariffs on Canadian steel and aluminum, Canada has responded with its own set of retaliatory tariffs. These new levies are substantial, with rates reaching as high as 50%.

Impacted Industries

  • Steel and Aluminum
  • Paper Products
  • Food Items
  • Apparel such as T-shirts

As tensions rise, Bank of England Governor Mark Carney has warned about the potential costs of these trade disputes. He noted that trade wars could disrupt global supply chains, raise prices for consumers, and create uncertainty that could dampen investment. Meanwhile, Canadian officials aim to pressure the US into reconsidering its trade policies. US authorities, however, remain resolute, framing these measures as necessary to address perceived unfair trade practices.
