Divorce, smartphones and control - BBC sees how Taliban run Afghanistan

Five years after the return of the Taliban to power, Afghanistan stands as a transformed nation. The BBC, in an exclusive series, delves into the lives of the Taliban hierarchy, revealing their methods as they mold Afghanistan's future. With a strong focus on traditional values, the Taliban have established strict regulations to control various aspects of Afghan life. The pervasive presence of smartphones and social media has proven challenging for the authorities, who seek to maintain ideological purity amid rapid technological change. Divorce rates, once settled quietly within communities, are now more openly discussed, as the Taliban enforce their interpretations of Sharia law to adjudicate matters. The group's leadership, many of whom have returned from exile, are keen on demonstrating governance aligned with their beliefs but face difficulties in addressing Afghanistan's economic woes. With international engagement limited, the Taliban have a narrow path to tread as they look to secure the nation under their rule.
