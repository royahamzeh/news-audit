Russia's second-biggest online retailer targeted in Ukrainian strikes

Ukrainian Strikes Target Ozon Warehouses

Overnight, Ukrainian forces launched strikes against Ozon, Russia's second-largest online retailer, causing disruptions in the regions of Dagestan and Krasnodar.

Impact on Operations

  • Ozon's logistics and supply chain operations are significantly affected.
  • The strikes aimed to undermine Russian e-commerce infrastructure.
  • The incident marks a rare targeting of civilian corporate entities.

Looking Ahead

Ozon is assessing the damage to recalibrate its operations to maintain service commitments. Further statements are awaited from Ozon and Russian authorities regarding the full impact and future actions.
