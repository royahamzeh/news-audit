Russia says at least seven killed in largest Ukrainian attack of 2026

In a significant escalation of the conflict, Russia reported that at least seven people were killed in what it described as the largest Ukrainian attack on Russian soil since the war began. The latest hostilities come amidst continued tensions and ongoing military engagements between the two countries. The Russian Ministry of Defense confirmed the casualties and stated that critical infrastructure in the affected areas had suffered severe damage. This attack, they alleged, was aimed at destabilizing the region further, and attributed the strikes to advanced Ukrainian artillery systems recently acquired from Western allies. Meanwhile, in retaliation, Moscow launched a series of deadly strikes targeting strategic locations in Ukraine, including the capital, Kyiv. Eyewitnesses reported intense fire and significant destruction across several districts in the city, with Ukrainian emergency services actively working to contain the resulting blazes and assist those affected. The Ukrainian government has not officially commented on the alleged attack within Russia but reaffirmed its commitment to defending its sovereignty against any aggression. This latest escalation underscores the volatility of the ongoing conflict, raising fears of a broader military confrontation. International reactions are being closely monitored as the situation continues to develop rapidly, with calls for de-escalation and diplomatic interventions intensifying.
