US sailors arrive in Thailand's Pattaya after record 250 days at sea

USS Abraham Lincoln Arrives in Pattaya

After spending an unprecedented 250 days at sea, the crew of the USS Abraham Lincoln has finally set foot in Thailand, docking near the lively city of Pattaya.

Pattaya: A City of Lights and Nightlife

  • Renowned for its vibrant nightlife
  • Notoriously known as a hub for sex tourism
  • Offers eager sailors a contrasting experience to sea life

Opportunities for Relaxation and Exploration

The sailors, following their long deployment, have ample opportunity to unwind and revel in Pattaya's diverse offerings. From neon-lit streets to cultural landmarks, the city provides a multitude of activities.

  • Nine months at sea culminates in a long-awaited shore leave
  • Pattaya offers burgeoning beaches and entertainment districts
  • Local economy poised to benefit from the sailors' presence
