Yemen's Mokha Port Captured by Houthis

The strategic port of Mokha on the Red Sea has fallen under the control of Yemen's Houthi rebels. This move brings the Iran-backed faction closer to dominating the Bab el-Mandeb strait, a crucial passage in international shipping.

  • Location: Red Sea
  • Significance: Historical trade port, now strategic for shipping routes
  • Impact: Increased leverage for Iran and the Houthis over maritime traffic

The port, once famous for coffee, now becomes a focal point in the region's complex political and military landscape.
