Berlin Pride Ramming Attack

  • Incident: A car was driven into a crowd at a Pride event in Berlin on Saturday night.
  • Time and Place: The attack occurred around 8 PM during the city's annual Pride celebration.
  • Response: Emergency services quickly responded, treating the injured on site.
  • Casualties: Several individuals sustained injuries; no fatalities reported yet.
  • Investigation: Police are on a manhunt for the suspect and exploring all motives, including hate crime possibilities.
  • Community Reaction: The attack has cast a shadow over the festivities; community leaders urge resilience.
  • Official Response: Berlin's mayor condemned the attack and pledged support to ensure the community's safety.
