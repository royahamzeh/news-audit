'I've never been to Afghanistan': Six million deportees forced to start over under the Taliban

Returnees Face Daunting Challenges in Afghanistan

Millions of Afghans forced to leave Pakistan and Iran now face an uncertain future in a country they hardly know. As of August, an additional million deportees were sent back this year, bringing the total number to approximately six million.

Key Challenges for Returnees

  • Identity Crisis: Many have never been to Afghanistan, complicating their sense of belonging.
  • Economic Strain: Job opportunities are limited under the Taliban regime.
  • Social Integration: Lack of familiar support networks hinders their ability to assimilate.

Humanitarian Response and Future Prospects

International aid organizations are providing basic assistance, but resources are scarce. Developing sustainable reintegration plans is imperative to ensure these deportees can contribute to society.
