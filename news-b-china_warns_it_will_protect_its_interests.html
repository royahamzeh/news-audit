China warns it will protect its interests after US widens sanctions against Iran

China's Response to US Sanctions on Iran

China has issued a warning to protect its interests following the United States' recent decision to widen sanctions against Iran, a move aimed at isolating Tehran due to its nuclear program.

US Sanctions

  • Targeted at countries engaging in trade with Iran
  • Intensified due to Iran's nuclear activities

China's Viewpoint

  • China buys significant quantities of Iranian oil
  • Critical of US unilateral sanctions
  • Commitment to maintaining economic ties with Iran

Global Implications

  • Presents additional friction in US-China relations
  • Impacts China's Belt and Road Initiative
Country Position
US Expanding sanctions
China Opposing unilateral sanctions
