Israel rejects Trump's 15-point plan for Gaza, Netanyahu says

In a recent development, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has firmly dismissed former U.S. President Donald Trump's proposed 15-point plan for addressing the ongoing conflict in Gaza. Netanyahu's primary rationale for this rejection is a staunch belief in ensuring the complete disarmament of Hamas before any potential military withdrawal from the region. According to Netanyahu, any resolution that doesn't guarantee the genuine and lasting disarmament of Hamas would leave Israel vulnerable to future threats from the militant group. Trump's plan, details of which have not been fully disclosed, was reportedly aimed at bringing about a sustainable ceasefire and a political solution to the fraught situation. However, Israel's security apparatus and political leadership remain unconvinced of its efficacy. The rejection of the plan underscores the complexity and the deeply entrenched nature of issues in Gaza, where previous attempts at ceasefire and negotiations have repeatedly faltered due to ongoing hostilities. Netanyahu's government maintains that any peace process must involve robust mechanisms to prevent Hamas from rearming and resuming its attacks on Israeli territories. As tensions continue to simmer, international stakeholders are closely monitoring the region for any changes in strategy or new developments that could alter the current trajectory of this protracted conflict.
