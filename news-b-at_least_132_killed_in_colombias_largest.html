At least 132 killed in Colombia's largest earthquake in years

Devastating Earthquake Strikes Colombia

In one of the most catastrophic natural disasters to hit Colombia in years, at least 132 people have lost their lives due to a massive earthquake that has shaken multiple regions of the country.

Casualties and Damage

  • 132 confirmed dead
  • Over 480 injured
  • Many feared trapped under debris

Rescue and Relief Efforts

The rescue operations are underway with teams working around the clock to reach those trapped under the rubble. Emergency services have been stretched thin as they attempt to address the widespread damage.

Government and International Response

  • State of emergency declared
  • Local and international aid agencies involved
  • Global messages of support

Economic and Community Impact

The earthquake has not only caused human loss but is also expected to have a significant economic impact due to the extensive property and infrastructure damage. The communities affected are in dire need of support as they work towards recovery.
