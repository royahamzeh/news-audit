Carney calls Trump's fresh tariffs a 'miscalculation' after trade talks collapse

Carney's Concerns on Trump's Tariffs

Mark Carney expressed concern over President Trump's imposition of new tariffs on Canadian goods, calling it a 'miscalculation.'

Trudeau's Response

  • Justin Trudeau announced retaliatory tariffs on US imports.
  • Trudeau stated the move was forced upon Canada.
  • Emphasized desire to avoid a trade war.

Economic and Political Impact

The breakdown of trade talks has led to strained US-Canada relations. Economists warn of potential disruptions to supply chains and increased consumer costs. Political analysts suggest Trump’s tactics could inadvertently strengthen the resolve of Canadian leadership.
