Unprecedented Rainfall Strikes Japan

Japan is grappling with the effects of an unprecedented rainfall that has tragically claimed the lives of eight people.

Impact on Infrastructure

  • Power outages affected over 20,000 households.
  • Approximately 7,000 passengers were stranded at Tokyo's Narita airport due to disruption in flights.

Flooding and Evacuations

The storm's intensity led to severe flooding in several regions, necessitating evacuations and activating emergency services.

Government Response

The government has issued advisories urging residents to move to safer locations and remain updated with the latest alerts
