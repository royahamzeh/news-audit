Hamas says it has agreed to disarm after Trump announces Board of Peace plan

In a significant development, a senior Hamas official has announced that the group has agreed to the Board of Peace's plan for disarmament following a proposal put forward by former U.S. President Donald Trump. This unexpected move has raised hopes for a potential thaw in Israeli-Palestinian relations, though Israel has yet to officially respond to the announcement. The plan, dubbed as the 'Board of Peace,' aims to foster long-term stability in the region by encouraging militant groups in Gaza to lay down their arms in exchange for economic aid and political concessions. Trump's involvement has been crucial, as he facilitated discussions between various stakeholders to come to an agreement. Hamas's decision to agree to the terms marks a major shift in their long-held stance and could pave the way for renewed dialogue. Despite this development, skepticism remains as both parties have a long history of conflict and failed agreements. The next steps involve the verification of compliance with the disarmament and the need for Israel to come to the negotiating table. International observers have emphasized the importance of transparency and goodwill from both sides to make this plan a success.
