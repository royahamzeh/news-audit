Meta fined $567m in largest child safety ruling against social media giant

Meta Faces Historic Fine in Child Safety Case

Meta has been ordered to pay a landmark fine, emphasizing the growing concerns over child safety across social media platforms.

  • Fine Details: New fine of $567 million added to existing $375 million, totaling $942 million.
  • Regulatory Concerns: Questions raised over user privacy and content monitoring for minors.
  • Meta's Response: Pledge to improve safety features and transparency.
