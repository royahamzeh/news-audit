Zelensky to press Trump on air defences while in Washington

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky is set to engage with former US President Donald Trump during his visit to Washington, aiming to strengthen Ukraine's air defense capabilities. Amid increasing Russian aggression and intensified missile attacks on Ukraine, Zelensky is looking to secure firm commitments from the United States to aid in bolstering Kyiv's anti-ballistic missile systems. Ukraine's current air defense is a cornerstone of its national defense strategy, crucial in countering the escalating hostilities from Russia. Zelensky's visit underscores the ongoing geopolitical tension and highlights the significant role the US plays in maintaining global peace and stability. Zelensky is expected to advocate for advanced defense systems that could deter impending threats. His deliberations with Trump will likely delve into expediting military aid and enhancing defense cooperation between Ukraine and the US. Russian forces' persistent attacks have necessitated swift action in shoring up defensive capabilities. The Ukrainian president’s diplomatic efforts in Washington seek to unify and secure crucial support against the backdrop of continuing conflict. This engagement reflects Zelensky's strategic initiative to safeguard Ukraine's sovereignty and fortify its defense infrastructure against external threats. The outcome of these discussions could play a pivotal role in the direction of Ukraine's security policy in the immediate future.
