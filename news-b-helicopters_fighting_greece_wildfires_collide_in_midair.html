Tragic Mid-Air Collision of Firefighting Helicopters in Greece

In a heartbreaking event, two helicopters engaged in firefighting efforts collided mid-air over Greece. Video footage shows one helicopter striking the propellers of the other, which subsequently bursts into flames. This incident highlights the dangers of aerial firefighting, particularly in challenging and smoky conditions.

  • Location: Greece
  • Type of Incident: Mid-air collision
  • Involved: Two firefighting helicopters
  • Outcome: Burst into flames, investigation ongoing

Greek fire services had deployed several helicopters to combat the devastating wildfires threatening multiple areas. The accident underscores the perilous nature of such missions. Rescue operations are in progress, and inquiries are underway to ascertain the collision's cause.
