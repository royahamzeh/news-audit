Nepal says it will need billions in funding to rebuild after flash floods

Nepal's Urgent Need for Reconstruction Funds

Nepal is in dire need of financial support following catastrophic flash floods that have devastated the nation, as officials report the necessity of billions in funding for recovery.

Flash Flood Impact

  • Widespread damage to homes, roads, and bridges.
  • Thousands of families displaced.
  • Immediate and long-term rebuilding needed.

Government Strategy

  • Mobilizing resources.
  • Coordinating relief efforts with international aid.
  • Strategic planning for sustainable reconstruction.

Challenges Faced

  • Limited access to affected areas due to road damage.
  • Need for immediate relief and long-term infrastructure development.
  • Balancing restoration with future risk mitigation.
