Three dead and several injured in shooting at food festival near Seattle's Space Needle

In a tragic incident at a food festival near Seattle's iconic Space Needle, three people lost their lives while several others, including a two-year-old boy, sustained injuries. Authorities quickly responded to the scene, securing the area and providing medical assistance to the injured. Seattle Mayor Katie Wilson confirmed that a suspect is now in custody, although the motivation behind the attack remains unclear. Eyewitnesses described scenes of chaos as festival-goers sought safety amidst the unfolding violence. The community is rallying to support the victims' families, with local businesses and citizens donating blood and funds to assist with medical expenses. Seattle's law enforcement is conducting a thorough investigation, hoping to provide insights into this senseless act. The tragedy has sparked conversations about safety protocols at public events and gun violence in the United States.
