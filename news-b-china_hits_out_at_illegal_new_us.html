China hits out at 'illegal' new US sanctions on Iran and trading partners

China Condemns US Sanctions on Iran

China has criticized new US sanctions on Iran, calling them 'illegal'. These sanctions target Iran's oil trade, which involves major dealings with China.

US Threatens Isolation

The United States has threatened to isolate countries that continue to trade with Tehran. This move primarily affects nations like China, a major buyer of Iranian oil.

China's Response

  • China calls the sanctions a violation of international norms.
  • Beijing urges adherence to multilateral agreements.
  • China maintains commitment to economic ties with Iran.
