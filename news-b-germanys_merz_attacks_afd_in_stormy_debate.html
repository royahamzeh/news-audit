Germany's Merz attacks AfD in stormy debate after far-right election win

Germany's Political Tensions: Merz vs AfD

  • Event: Intense parliamentary debate.
  • Main Figures: Chancellor Friedrich Merz and AfD leader Alice Weidel.
  • Key Topic: Migration policies and their impact.

Chancellor’s Accusation

During a heated session in Germany’s parliament, Chancellor Friedrich Merz accused AfD leader Alice Weidel of promoting 'ethnic cleansing' through her party's migration policy. Merz argued that such policies could potentially strip Germany of its skilled workforce, which is crucial for the country's industrial and economic prowess.

AfD’s Defense

Alice Weidel defended the AfD's stance, claiming it prioritizes national security and employment for German citizens. This comes in the wake of the AfD's recent electoral victories, showcasing its rising influence among voters.
