US Envoys Meet Zelensky Following Talks with Putin

US diplomatic efforts took a significant step forward as envoys held talks in Kyiv with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky. This followed crucial discussions with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Key Highlights

  • Encouragement: Steve Witkoff, leader of the US delegation, reported feeling "encouraged" by the discussions.
  • Substantive Talks: Described as "substantive," the talks focused on resolving the ongoing conflict.
  • Diplomatic Aim: To de-escalate tensions between Ukraine and Russia.

These talks are part of a broader US strategy to engage directly with both leaders, seeking peaceful resolutions and highlighting the importance of communication channels.
