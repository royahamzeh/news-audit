Five Years of Taliban Rule: Transforming Afghanistan

Five years have lapsed since the Taliban's re-ascent to power in Afghanistan. A BBC exclusive series follows the inner workings of the group's leadership.

Key Changes and Challenges

  • Traditional Values: The Taliban enforces strict regulations reflecting their interpretation of Sharia law.
  • Technological Impact: The rise of smartphones and social media challenges the group's control efforts.
  • Divorce and Social Norms: Divorce, once quietly resolved, is now subject to their governance policies.
  • Governance and Economic Hurdles: Leadership faces economic challenges amid limited international involvement.
