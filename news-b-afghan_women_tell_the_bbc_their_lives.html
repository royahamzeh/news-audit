Afghan women tell the BBC their lives are unrecognisable after five years of Taliban rule

The Reality Under Taliban Rule

Five years after the Taliban's return, Afghan women are grappling with unprecedented changes that have redefined their lives.

Impact on Daily Life

  • Public Punishments: Women are subjected to public floggings for infractions like violating dress codes.
  • Employment Barriers: Many have lost jobs, stripping them of financial independence and purpose.
  • Education Setbacks: Schools and universities have limited access for girls, halting their education.
  • Healthcare Challenges: Restrictions on healthcare access put lives, particularly during childbirth, at risk.

Broader Implications

The international community remains concerned over the severe infringement on personal freedoms experienced by Afghan women. Their narratives paint a vivid picture of hardships under Taliban rule, where progress has regressed to a distant memory. There is a growing call for global attention and intervention to address the alarming human rights situation.
