UN Warns of 'Supersized' El Niño

The United Nations has issued a warning regarding an impending 'supersized' El Niño, expected to significantly impact global economies. The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) has indicated that this natural weather event could result in substantial economic disruptions worldwide.

Key Impacts of El Niño

  • Characterized by warming of sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern Pacific Ocean.
  • Potential to trigger severe droughts in Australia and Southeast Asia.
  • Could lead to intense rainfall and flooding in the Americas.
  • Prompts variability in weather patterns across the globe.

Preparation and Mitigation

This year's El Niño is anticipated to be particularly intense, urging global governments to prepare for its socio-economic effects. The WMO stresses the importance of readiness plans to mitigate impacts on agriculture, water supplies, and infrastructure.
