Spain's PM blames traffickers after 60,000 migrants reach Ceuta from Morocco

Spain's Response to Migrant Influx in Ceuta

Spain's Prime Minister has declared the recent surge of migrants to Ceuta as an "attack" on their territory, attributing it to the actions of human traffickers. The estimated 60,000 migrants arriving from Morocco have triggered a swift response from the Spanish government.

Blame on Traffickers

  • The Prime Minister blames human traffickers for the sudden increase in migrant numbers.
  • Ceuta has seen an alarming influx of 60,000 migrants from Morocco.

Government Action

  • All illegal entrants will be returned to Morocco, according to Spain's stated policy.
  • Spain seeks international cooperation to address the root causes of migration.

Humanitarian Concerns

  • Human rights organizations call for a humane approach to dealing with migrants.
  • There is an emphasis on providing legal pathways to asylum and necessary aid.
