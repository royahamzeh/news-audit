Door was locked at Pakistan hospital where fire killed 14 babies, witnesses say

Tragedy Strikes Pakistan Hospital

A fire broke out in a Pakistani hospital's neonatal ward, resulting in the death of 14 infants. Witnesses allege the ward door was locked, hindering evacuation. However, a hospital official claims the door was manned by security, not locked, to ensure safety. This discrepancy has sparked outrage and demands for accountability from affected families.

Public Reaction

  • Outrage over conflicting reports about the ward door's status
  • Calls for a thorough investigation to determine potential lapses
  • Demand for improved safety protocols in hospitals

Investigations and Reactions

The hospital has pledged full cooperation with investigations. Government officials and the public call for reforms in safety regulations to prevent future incidents. The tragedy emphasizes the need for robust safety measures in medical facilities.
