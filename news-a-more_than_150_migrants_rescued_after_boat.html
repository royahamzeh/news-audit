More than 150 migrants rescued after boat catches fire in Channel

In a dramatic rescue operation, more than 150 migrants were saved after their boat caught fire while attempting to cross the English Channel towards the United Kingdom. According to French authorities, the engine room of the vessel caught fire, putting the lives of the passengers, who were mostly seeking asylum, at great risk. The incident was reported in the early hours of the morning, leading to an immediate coordinated response from both French and British rescue services. Patrol boats and helicopters, equipped for maritime emergencies, quickly arrived at the scene. The rescue teams braved challenging conditions to transfer the passengers safely to shore. Officials confirmed that all victims were unharmed, although many were being treated for shock and mild hypothermia. The incident has once again brought attention to the ongoing crisis of risky migrant crossings in the Channel, a narrow yet perilous route for those seeking a new life in the UK. Charities and humanitarian organizations have reiterated calls for safer and legal pathways for asylum seekers to avert such dangers. This rescue underscores the urgent need for international cooperation and comprehensive solutions to address the underlying issues driving migrants to make these desperate journeys.
