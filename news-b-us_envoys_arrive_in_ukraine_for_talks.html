US Envoys' Diplomatic Mission to Ukraine

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner have reached Ukraine as part of their diplomatic mission, following their discussions with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Highlights of the Trip

  • Engaged in high-level discussions with President Putin on regional security.
  • Productive talks aimed at exploring cooperation opportunities.
  • Arrival in Kyiv on Sunday to confer with Ukrainian officials.

Objectives in Kyiv

  • Convey outcomes of Moscow meetings to Ukrainian leaders.
  • Discuss strategies to bolster Ukraine's economic and security resilience.
  • Reaffirm US support for Ukraine's sovereignty.
