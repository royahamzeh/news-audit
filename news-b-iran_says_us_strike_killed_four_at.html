Iran Accuses US of 'War Crime' in Alleged Wedding Attack

In a significant escalation of tensions, Iran has accused the United States of a 'war crime' after a reported airstrike killed four people, including two children, at a wedding.

Details of the Incident

  • Iranian media allege shrapnel hit a home during a wedding.
  • Iran responded with missile and drone attacks on US targets in the Middle East.

US Response

  • The US denies targeting civilians, stressing adherence to international law.
  • Pentagon expresses condolences for any loss of life and commits to investigating the incident.

International Reaction

  • Iran calls for international condemnation of US actions and vows retaliation.
  • Global leaders urge restraint and dialogue to prevent further escalation.
