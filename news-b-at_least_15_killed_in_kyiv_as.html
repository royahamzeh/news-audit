Kyiv Under Siege: A Devastating Attack

In a tragic turn of events, at least 15 people have been killed following a "massive attack" on Kyiv, with blocks of flats and warehouses as direct targets. Ukrainian officials confirmed this assault on Thursday, casting a shadow on the city's security infrastructure.
