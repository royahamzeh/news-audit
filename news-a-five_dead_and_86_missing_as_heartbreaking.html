Five dead and 86 missing as 'heartbreaking' fire breaks out on Philippines ferry

A tragic incident unfolded when a ferry caught fire near the popular tourist destination of Coron in the Palawan province of the Philippines. The blaze, described by officials as 'heartbreaking,' has resulted in the confirmed deaths of five individuals, with a distressing 86 people reported missing. The fire erupted as the vessel was approaching its destination, which is renowned for its picturesque landscapes and tourist attractions. Emergency response teams have been deployed to the scene, but the scale of the tragedy has reportedly overwhelmed local resources. Witnesses described scenes of chaos as passengers scrambled to evacuate the burning ferry. Rescue operations are ongoing, with authorities prioritizing locating and assisting survivors amid the heartbreaking devastation. The cause of the fire is yet to be determined, and an investigation is underway. The incident has cast a somber shadow over Coron, an area heavily reliant on tourism, as families of the missing await news of their loved ones. The nation's leadership has expressed deep sorrow over the loss and promised support for both rescue operations and those affected by the disaster. As the search continues, the hope remains that more survivors might still be found, though concerns grow as time passes without new findings.
